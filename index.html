      About Isometric World

      In January 2026, Andy Coenen released Isometric NYC: all of New York in SimCity 2000-style pixel art, redrawn tile by tile by an image model. People around the world have since made maps of their own cities, by AI, by code and by hand. This page puts all the ones we could find on a single isometric Earth.

      Reading the map

      • Each little city is a place with at least one isometric map. Its landmark and its buildings hint at the real city, and its skyline grows with the number of maps.
      • Click a city to see its maps. Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, and use the turn buttons (or Q and E) to rotate the world a quarter turn, the way city builders do.
      • The colours show how each map was made: AI-painted Drawn from data 3D diorama Classic

      Add a map

      Know one that is missing? Every entry is one object in data/projects.json in the diocosta/isometric-world repository. Open a pull request with yours, or open an issue with the link, the city and who made it.
